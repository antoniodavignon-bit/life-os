# 👤 User Personas

---

## Persona 1 — The Overwhelmed Builder

**Who they are:**  
A solopreneur, side-hustler, or early-stage entrepreneur juggling too many ideas with no consistent execution system.

**Demographics:**  
- Age: 22–38  
- Platform: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram  
- Income: $0–$3k/month from their business  

**Pain Points:**
- Has 10 business ideas but can't commit to executing one
- Feels productive but has no revenue to show for it
- Wants financial stability but can't create repeatable income

**What they've tried:**
- Motivational content
- Time-blocking apps
- Starting a journal

**What they actually need:**
- A daily structure that forces revenue tasks first
- A system that doesn't require willpower to maintain

**How Life OS speaks to them:**  
> "Stop being busy. Start being profitable. Here's the 9-task system."

---

## Persona 2 — The Restart Loop User

**Who they are:**  
Someone who starts projects, builds momentum for 2–3 weeks, then stops. Repeats every few months with a new idea or renewed energy.

**Demographics:**  
- Age: 25–42  
- Platform: Reddit, Twitter/X, YouTube  
- Income: Stable job income, $0–$500/month side income  

**Pain Points:**
- Knows what to do but doesn't do it consistently
- Mistakes motivation for a system
- Loses momentum when external hype dies down

**What they've tried:**
- Goal-setting frameworks (OKRs, vision boards)
- Productivity apps (Notion, Todoist)
- "Accountability partners"

**What they actually need:**
- A system that runs on structure, not energy
- A daily protocol that doesn't depend on mood
- A feedback loop that shows them what's working

**How Life OS speaks to them:**  
> "You don't need more motivation. You need a system that runs without it."
